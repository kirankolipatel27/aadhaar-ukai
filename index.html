Our Aadhaar Services

The following Aadhaar updation and child enrollment services are available at Ukai Dam Sub Post Office:

Service 1

Name Spelling Correction

Correction of minor spelling errors or name matching with official supporting identity proof as per UIDAI norms.

Service 2

Address Update / Correction

Updating current residential address with eligible original proof of address (POA) documents.

Service 3

Mobile Number Update

Linking or updating your active mobile number to receive OTPs for government services and verify Aadhaar online.

Service 4

Fingerprint / Biometric Update

Re-capturing or updating fingerprints and iris scans for mandatory biometric updates or authentication accuracy.

Service 5

Photo Update / Change

Updating the photograph printed on the Aadhaar card directly at the Post Office biometric update station.

Service 6 (New Enrollment)

Child Enrollment (0 to 5 Years)

First-time new Aadhaar enrollment for children aged 0 to 5 years (Baal Aadhaar) with birth certificate and parent's Aadhaar.
